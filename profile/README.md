## 🚀 About Us

At Saral Startup School, we're passionate about empowering individuals to become startup leaders in the current ecosystem. We're not just another tech school — we believe successful startups are built on more than just coding ability, and anyone can thrive in this dynamic space given the right guidance.

Before the pandemic, technology startups were already disrupting industries and creating demand for a new breed of professionals. When the pandemic hit, the demand for people who can **adapt, innovate, and navigate uncertainty** became paramount. During this time, we at Saral Startup School realized a critical gap — a need for **accessible education** that equips individuals with the skills and knowledge to thrive in the dynamic startup ecosystem.

Our mission is to empower individuals to become well-rounded leaders who not only develop cutting-edge solutions but also understand the intricate workings of the startup world.
